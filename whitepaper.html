// Section 01

The compliance gap.

The EU AI Act creates binding obligations for organisations deploying AI systems in high-risk categories — including credit scoring, employment tools, critical infrastructure, and law enforcement. These obligations include maintaining detailed logs of AI decision-making, ensuring human oversight, and demonstrating that systems operate within defined risk parameters.

The majority of organisations currently deploying AI have no mechanism to produce this evidence. Consultancy documents and policy frameworks describe what should be done. They do not provide the technical infrastructure to do it.

AILeash fills this gap. It is not a dashboard, a report, or a framework. It is live infrastructure — an API endpoint that sits between your AI system and its decisions, logging every action into a cryptographically chained audit record before the response is returned.

The enforcement timeline

High-risk AI system obligations under the EU AI Act apply from August 2026. Organisations found non-compliant face fines of up to €30 million or 6% of global annual turnover. The audit trail requirement is not optional — it is the mechanism by which compliance is demonstrated.

Technical architecture.

AILeash is built on pure Python with zero external runtime dependencies. The data layer uses SQLite in WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) mode for concurrent read performance. The audit chain is maintained in a dedicated table with each entry containing a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry — forming an unbroken Merkle chain from genesis block to present.

Request lifecycle

When a governance call arrives at POST /api/govern, the following occurs in under 10ms:

Incoming device_id + action + context
→

Each hash is computed over: previous_hash + decision + timestamp_ns + device_id + action

The response is returned to the caller with the decision, the new chain hash, the chain index, and the nanosecond timestamp. The full chain can be retrieved at any time via GET /api/audit/{device_id} and verified independently.

// Section 04

The Merkle audit chain.

A Merkle chain provides a mathematically provable guarantee of record integrity. Each entry in the chain contains a SHA-256 hash computed over the content of that entry plus the hash of the previous entry. This means:

Any modification to any historical record — no matter how small — produces a different hash, which breaks every subsequent entry in the chain. The break is immediately detectable. There is no way to alter a past record and repair the chain without knowing the original data for every subsequent entry.

What this means for regulators

A valid, unbroken chain is cryptographic proof that the audit log has not been altered since the genesis block. An organisation can hand this chain to a regulator with confidence that any tampering attempt would be detectable. This is a materially stronger guarantee than any conventional logging system.

// Section 05

Sovereign deployment.

Cloud AI governance tools introduce a fundamental problem: the data required to demonstrate compliance leaves the customer's network and is stored by a third party. This creates GDPR exposure, introduces vendor lock-in, and means the audit trail is only as trustworthy as the vendor's security posture.

AILeash is deployed on the customer's own hardware. The engine, the database, and the audit chain run within the customer's network perimeter. No customer data, no decision records, and no audit logs ever leave the customer's infrastructure.

Anthropic's infrastructure costs remain flat regardless of customer scale. There is no capacity bottleneck. The more devices a customer operates, the more value they extract from their own sovereign deployment.

GDPR alignment

Because data never leaves the customer's network, there is no cross-border data transfer to account for, no third-party data processor to audit, and no vendor dependency in the event of a regulatory investigation. The customer owns the evidence entirely.

Regulatory alignment.

AILeash is designed to satisfy the audit and transparency obligations introduced by the EU AI Act for high-risk AI systems, as well as supporting compliance with related frameworks.

EU AI Act — Art. 12

Record-keeping obligations for high-risk AI systems. AILeash provides automatic, tamper-evident logging of every AI-assisted decision with nanosecond timestamps.

EU AI Act — Art. 9

Risk management system requirements. AILeash's ALLOW / CHALLENGE / BLOCK framework provides the live risk control layer required.

GDPR — Art. 22

Automated individual decision-making. AILeash logs provide the audit trail needed to demonstrate human oversight and explainability of AI decisions.
