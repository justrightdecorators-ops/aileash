Stay Safe Online
🔒

Keep your location private

Never tell anyone online where you live, which school you go to or where you hang out. Even if they seem nice.

😱

Secrets are a red flag

If someone asks you to keep your conversations secret from your parents, that is a warning sign. Tell a trusted adult.

🎁

No real gifts online

If someone you only know online offers you gifts, money or free stuff — they want something in return. Tell your parents.

💬

Block feels good

Blocking someone who makes you uncomfortable is always the right move. You do not owe anyone your time online.

Something Happened?
If you feel unsafe, scared or someone has done something that feels wrong — you can get help right now. You are not in trouble.
✏ Call 999 — Emergency Report to CEOP Childline — 0800 1111
What counts as unsafe?
⚠

Someone asked to meet in person

If someone you met online wants to meet you in real life, tell your parents or a trusted adult immediately. Do not go alone.

⚠

Someone sent you a weird photo

If someone sent you a photo that made you uncomfortable, do not share it. Tell a trusted adult. You have done nothing wrong.

⚠

Someone is being nasty to you

Bullying online is serious. Screenshot it, block them, and tell a parent or teacher. You do not have to put up with it.

⚠

Someone asked you to keep a secret

Trustworthy people do not ask children to keep secrets. If this happened, tell your parents. They will not be angry at you.
