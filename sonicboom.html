02 — The SHA-256 Merkle Audit Chain

Every decision AILeash makes is sealed into a SHA-256 Merkle chain. This is the same cryptographic technology that underpins Bitcoin and modern blockchain infrastructure — except applied to AI compliance.

Each block in the chain contains the event data, the decision, the timestamp, and the hash of the previous block. This means every entry is mathematically linked to every entry before it. Change one record — even a single character — and the entire chain breaks. It is not difficult to detect tampering. It is impossible to tamper without detection.

This is not a log file. This is not a database record. This is cryptographic proof — verifiable by anyone, anywhere, at any time.

03 — The 9-Signal Weighted Scoring Engine

Every decision runs through nine weighted signals simultaneously. The engine does not guess. It does not use rules. It uses a mathematically calibrated scoring model with EWMA trust decay — meaning trust scores evolve over time based on behaviour history, not just the current event.

Signal 1 — Trust Score
EWMA trust decay. Each user builds a trust score over time. Block decisions reduce it fast. Allow decisions rebuild it slowly. Weight: 30%.
Signal 2 — Velocity 60s
Actions in the last 60 seconds. Burst attacks, bot behaviour, and scripted fraud all spike here. Weight: 15%.
Signal 3 — Velocity 5m / 1h
Medium and long-window velocity tracking. Catches slower attacks that evade 60-second detection. Combined weight: 20%.
Signal 4 — Transaction Amount
Log-scaled amount scoring. High-value transactions raise risk proportionally without over-penalising normal large purchases. Weight: 15%.
Signal 5 — Device Risk
Device fingerprint risk score passed in by the client platform. Rooted devices, emulators, and flagged hardware raise this signal. Weight: 10%.
Signal 6 — Anomaly Score
Behavioural anomaly score from the client platform. Unusual patterns, off-hours activity, and atypical usage all contribute. Weight: 10%.
Signal 7 — Country Shift
User suddenly appearing in a different country from their last session. Account takeover indicator. Weight: 10%.
Signal 8 — Safe Country
Request origin against a curated safe-country list. Non-listed countries add a configurable risk premium. Weight: 10%.
Signal 9 — Trust Floor
Low trust score adds a compounding penalty. Users who have previously triggered blocks face elevated scrutiny automatically.

06 — The Legal Landscape

High-risk AI systems must have continuous risk management, tamper-evident audit chains, explainable decisions, and human oversight. Fine: up to 3% of global annual turnover or 15 million euros per violation.

Online Safety Act 2023 — In Force Now

Ofcom is actively investigating platforms. Platforms must have documented, systematic, auditable risk assessment. A moderation team and a report button is not compliance. Ofcom can fine up to 10% of global turnover.

If children under 18 can access your platform — even inadvertently — the Childrens Code applies. Best interests of the child by default. No profiling unless strictly necessary. The ICO fined TikTok 12.7 million pounds for violations.

Platforms operating in Europe must demonstrate systemic risk assessment, algorithmic transparency, and evidence of risk mitigation. Very Large Online Platforms face full DSA obligations immediately. Smaller platforms must still demonstrate compliance.
