What Guardian actually does — in plain terms

Guardian is a tool your child chooses to use when a message feels wrong. They paste it in, and Guardian checks it against known grooming and manipulation patterns — secrecy requests, location requests, unusual flattery, gift offers, isolation tactics. It gives them a straight answer and a next step, plus direct links to CEOP, Childline and 999 if they need real help. It does not automatically read or monitor every message your child sends on WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat or any other app — no tool can do that without deep access most phones don't allow. Guardian works when your child brings something to it. Keep talking to your child directly — that's still the strongest protection there is.

What Guardian Checks For

The patterns predators use. Explained simply.

When your child pastes in a message, Guardian checks it against these known warning signs — instantly, and for free.

😱

Secrecy Requests

"Don't tell your parents" or "keep this between us" is one of the clearest warning signs there is. Guardian flags it immediately and tells your child why it matters.

Pattern check
📍

Location & Meet-Up Requests

Questions about where your child lives, goes to school, or requests to meet up are flagged with a clear explanation of why it's risky.

Pattern check
✨

Love Bombing & Flattery

Excessive praise, "you're not like other kids," or unusual attention from an adult online. Guardian explains why this pattern is used to build trust before harm.

Pattern check
🎁

Gift & Money Offers

Offers of gifts, money or "free stuff" from someone your child only knows online are flagged as a common manipulation tactic.

Pattern check
🔒

Isolation Language

Messages designed to pull a child away from parents or trusted adults ("they won't understand," "they'll be angry") are flagged as a serious warning sign.

Pattern check

Every check your child runs is hashed into a tamper-evident record, so if something serious needs reporting to CEOP or the police, there's a timestamped log of it.
