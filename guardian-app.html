How It Works

Two apps. Complete protection.

No app store. No contract. Install both PWAs in under five minutes. Guardian starts working immediately.

01

You install the Parent App

Visit sebbi.pro/guardian-parent on your phone. Sign up, add your children, add each child's name and generate their Guardian code. Your live dashboard is ready instantly.

02

Your child installs the Child App

Your child visits sebbi.pro/guardian-child on their phone and enters their Guardian code. They use it as their safe browser for social media and messaging. Guardian scans everything in real time.

03

You get alerts. They stay safe.

The moment Guardian detects grooming, suspicious contacts, isolation attempts or any threat you get an instant notification. Every alert is hashed into a tamper-proof evidence chain automatically.
