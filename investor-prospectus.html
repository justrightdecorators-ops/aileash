Block 04 — The Engineering

Built for one property: tamper-evidence.

Every design decision in this platform traces back to a single requirement — that an audit record, once written, cannot be altered without the alteration being immediately detectable. The Merkle-chain architecture links every decision to the one before it; breaking any single record breaks the chain visibly, not silently.

The licence system is air-gapped by design: HMAC-SHA256 signed tokens that don't depend on a live connection to validate, closing a common failure mode in compliance tooling that assumes continuous connectivity. It's been through 22 stress tests, not just a design review.

The engine itself runs a 9-signal real-time scoring model — trust, velocity, amount, device risk, behavioural anomaly, and geographic signals — returning a decision in a median 28ms in production. That number is not a benchmark run in isolation; it's measured on the live system, independently verified.
