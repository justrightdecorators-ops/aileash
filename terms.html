8.The sealed record

The point of sebbi.pro is a record nobody can quietly change. Please understand what that means before you send us anything:

  • Decisions and other records you send are sealed into an append-only hash chain. Once sealed, a record cannot be edited or deleted — not by you, not by us. That is a feature you are paying for.
  • The chain is timestamped externally (OpenTimestamps and Bitcoin) and witnessed by independent operators, so its history is held in places we do not control.
  • Some records and proofs are public by design so anyone can verify them. Public verification routes never reveal your personal data.
Do not send personal data into the chain. Use pseudonymous identifiers for people and devices (for example a hashed customer number, never a name, email address or phone number). Because the chain cannot forget, anything identifying you send into it stays there. Request details we record for the machine-proof report are kept off the chain and deleted after two years; only their fingerprints are sealed. See the Data Protection Statement.
