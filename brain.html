What It Is

A mathematical brain that decides what AI can and cannot do.

AILeash Brain is a cryptographic governance layer that sits between your AI system and the outside world. Every instruction your AI receives is converted into a SHA-256 hash and checked against a set of mathematically defined rules. Permitted instructions pass through in milliseconds. Blocked instructions are stopped instantly and the decision is permanently sealed in a Merkle audit chain that cannot be altered by anyone.

This is not a keyword filter. This is cryptographic governance. Every letter of every word contributes to a unique mathematical signature. Every decision is tamper-proof evidence.
