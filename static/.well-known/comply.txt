# DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS — how an append-only chain coexists with erasure
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
Erasure-Compatible: true
Erasure-Method: identifiers are fingerprinted on arrival, so the chain
  never holds a person's identity. Personal data is deleted in the
  operator's own systems as normal; what remains sealed is a hash that
  resolves to nothing.
DSR-Lifecycle-Sealed: received, assessed with reasoning, extended,
  completed
DSR-Deadline: fixed at the moment of receipt. An extension is its own
  sealed block, so it cannot be applied retrospectively to cover a date
  already missed.
