Why ai.txt Exists

The law already requires this.
The infrastructure didn't exist.

The EU AI Act, UK Online Safety Act, GDPR Article 22, and the Digital Services Act all mandate AI accountability. Until now, there was no universal, machine-readable way to declare it.

⚖️
Regulatory compliance, declared
Maps directly to EU AI Act Articles 9, 12, 13 and 14. Every field corresponds to a legal obligation. Regulators can verify compliance programmatically, not through a 200-page PDF.
🔗
Cryptographically verifiable
The AI-Chain-Tip field contains a live SHA-256 Merkle hash. Anyone — including a regulator — can verify the chain hasn't been tampered with at any point in history.
🌐
Infrastructure level adoption
Like robots.txt, ai.txt sits at the root of any domain. AI crawlers, compliance tools, and regulatory systems can read it automatically. No integration required.
🛡️
Sovereign by design
The AI-Sovereign field declares whether your AI audit data stays on your own hardware. Customer data never leaves your network. Passes GDPR data sovereignty requirements automatically.
